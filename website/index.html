The kinds of links it catches

A "dirty link" is any link that leads somewhere designed to take your money or your credentials. Overseer checks every one against known patterns and what other users have already reported.

Cloned bank & login links

Links to pages that copy a real bank or brokerage login pixel-for-pixel.

Fake checkout links

Payment pages that redirect through a lookalike gateway right before you submit.

Crypto & investment scam links

"Double your money" and fake exchange links shared in DMs and comments.

Malicious redirect chains

Links that bounce through several domains to hide where you'll really land.

Fake giveaway & prize links

"You've won" links designed to harvest card details or account access.

Links reported by the community

Anything another user has flagged as a scam, even before it hits any watchlist.

Questions
Frequently asked
Does Overseer cost anything?

No. Overseer is free to use, with no subscription and no features held back behind a paywall.

Does it slow down my browser?

It's not continuously scanning everything you do. It watches for specific financial moments — logins, payments, clipboard changes — so there's nothing running in the background most of the time.

What does it actually see?

Signals tied to financial activity: bank and payment domains, checkout forms, clipboard changes, autofill, and notification prompts. It isn't logging your general browsing.

What happens when it blocks something?

The risky action is held or stopped immediately, and a plain-language report is waiting for you next time you open the app — what happened, why, and what Overseer did about it.

How is the risk score calculated?

Each signal adds to a percentage score: +10% if the destination is a newly discovered page, +15% if the destination changed shortly after your input, +20% if it uses sendBeacon or fires without your knowledge, +40% if there's an outgoing request body, and +45% if the request is cross-origin. Below 40%, the action is allowed. From 40–70%, it's allowed but warned and logged. At 70% and above, it's blocked outright.

Do I need to configure anything?

No setup required. Overseer works out of the box once it's installed, and you can open it anytime to check its activity history.

Can I report a scam link myself?

Yes. Report any link in a few seconds — no account needed. Once it's confirmed, every other user gets warned automatically before they open it.

Is my data sent anywhere?

Only what's needed to evaluate a specific risky moment gets analyzed — Overseer isn't built to collect or sell your browsing data.
